Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026
The short version: nothing you save is sent to us, because ReelFile has no server and no account system.

What ReelFile stores on your device

  • The links you save.
  • Optional titles you type for them.
  • Folder names you create.
  • Preview image addresses fetched from the platform the reel came from.
  • Your choice of folder icon and colour.

This lives in the app's own storage on your device, including a private container shared with the ReelFile Share Extension. It is not transmitted to the developer. Deleting a reel removes it; deleting the app removes everything.

What ReelFile does not collect

  • No account, name, email or phone number.
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not receive any.

Network requests

To turn a shared link into something playable, ReelFile contacts the platform that hosts the reel — Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or Facebook — to resolve the link, look up a preview image, and load the platform's player. Those requests go directly from your device to that platform.

Like any web request, they include information the platform needs to answer, such as your IP address and a browser user agent. That information goes to the platform, not to us, and is handled under that platform's own privacy policy. ReelFile does not add identifiers to those requests.

If you choose Sign in to watch for a video that requires an account, the platform's login page opens in a private in-app browser. Your credentials are entered on the platform's own page; ReelFile never sees or stores them.

Children

ReelFile is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your choices

  • Delete any saved reel from its menu.
  • Delete a whole folder; its reels move to Unsorted.
  • Delete the app to remove all locally stored data.
