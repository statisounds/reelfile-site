Support

ReelFile keeps the reels you want to see again in folders on your own device. It works on iPhone, iPad and Android.

Saving a reel

iPhone / iPad

  1. In Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or Facebook, tap Share on the reel.
  2. Choose Save to ReelFile from the Share Sheet.
  3. Pick a folder (or create one) and tap Save.

Android

  1. In Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or Facebook, tap Share on the reel.
  2. Choose ReelFile from the Share menu.
  3. Pick a folder (or create one) and tap Save.

You can also add a link by hand from a folder's ⋯ menu → Add link. On Android, a reel link opened from a browser can be sent to ReelFile with Open with.

Common questions

A reel shows a grey card with no picture.
That platform does not publish a preview image for that post, usually because it is private or limited. The reel still works from its own app.

A video says "This reel needs a Facebook account".
Some Facebook posts are only shown to signed-in members. Tap Sign in to watch to open Facebook's own login page in a private browser, or Watch on Facebook to open it in the Facebook app. ReelFile never sees your password.

The video will not play.
Some platforms block playback outside their own apps, and some posts get removed. Use the Open in … button to watch it in the original app. On Android, make sure Android System WebView is up to date.

Can I change a folder's icon?
Yes — long-press the folder (or open it and use ⋯) → Change icon. Pick any symbol and colour, or choose Use automatic.

Where is my data stored?
On iOS, in the app's private storage. If Sync with iCloud is on (Settings, from the gear on the home page), folders and reels are also kept in your own iCloud account so an iPhone and iPad match. Apple holds that copy; the developer cannot read it, and you can turn syncing off at any time. On Android, everything stays on the device and backups are disabled. Deleting the app removes the local copy; a synced library comes back when you reinstall.

I bought the unlock on iPad. How do I get my library on iPhone?
The unlock already follows your Apple Account. For the library, sign in to the same iCloud account on both devices and leave Sync with iCloud on — they reconcile within a few seconds. If iCloud isn't an option, open Settings → Transfer, tap Export library on one device, send the .reelfile file to the other (AirDrop or Files), and choose Import library. Importing merges, so nothing already on the device is replaced.

I already had different folders on my iPhone and iPad before the update. What happens?
They merge. The first time each device opens the updated app, it adds what the other one has; nothing is replaced or deleted. Two details worth knowing: if both devices have a folder with the same name, they stay separate (two "Music" tiles) because ReelFile cannot know they were meant to be one — rename one, or move its reels across and delete the spare. Likewise, a reel you saved on both devices appears once per device it was saved on. A free library can also end up past the free plan — 10 reels, 2 folders and 1 subfolder; everything already saved is kept, and the limit only applies to new saves.

Can I move just one folder to another device?
Yes. Long-press the folder — or open it and use ⋯ — then choose Transfer folder. ReelFile saves that folder, everything nested inside it, and their reels as a .reelfile you can AirDrop or keep in Files. Sending a folder is part of the Unlimited unlock; the whole-library Settings → Transfer remains free, so moving your data never depends on buying anything.

Does it work offline?
Your library, folders and previews are available offline. Playing a reel needs a connection, because the video streams from the platform.

The app icon has a coloured ring on my home screen.
That ring is drawn by your launcher, not by ReelFile. Launchers normalise app icons with their own frame or plate; it is not part of the app's icon. ReelFile's icon itself has no ring (you can see it on the Android App info screen). Changing the launcher's icon style/frames or using a different launcher will change it.
