Support

ReelFile keeps the reels you want to see again in folders on your own device.

Saving a reel

  1. In Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or Facebook, tap Share on the reel.
  2. Choose ReelFile from the Share Sheet.
  3. Pick a folder (or create one) and tap Save.

You can also add a link by hand from a folder's ⋯ menu → Add link.

Common questions

A reel shows a grey card with no picture

That platform does not publish a preview image for that post, usually because it is private or limited. The reel still works from its own app.

A video says "This reel needs a Facebook account"

Some Facebook posts are only shown to signed-in members. Tap Sign in to watch to open Facebook's own login page in a private browser, or Watch on Facebook to open it in the Facebook app. ReelFile never sees your password.

The video will not play

Some platforms block playback outside their own apps, and some posts get removed. Use the Open in … button to watch it in the original app.

Can I change a folder's icon?

Yes — long-press the folder (or open it and use ⋯) → Change icon. Pick any symbol and colour, or choose Use automatic.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes your library.

Does it work offline?

Your library, folders and previews are available offline. Playing a reel needs a connection, because the video streams from the platform.
